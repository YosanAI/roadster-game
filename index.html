LEVEL 01 / THE WALL

Stick the landing.

  1. Drive. Hold W or the pedal to accelerate, and S or the brake to slow down. Suction keeps the wheels on the road, the wall, and the inverted lip. You control the climb.
  2. Leave the edge. Keep driving along the top. When the wheels leave the wall, the controls automatically switch to thrusters.
  3. Roll upright. Hold Q or E to fire the lift pair on one side. Release to let the opposite pair check the rotation. Get your wheels underneath you.
  4. Land softly. Hold SPACE to fire all four lift jets. They push in the car’s up direction, with exhaust below the wheels. Use short pulses to slow the fall, and W/S or the up/down arrows to control forward/backward drift. The computer checks sideways drift automatically; green LEFT / RIGHT controls show its corrections. Hold A/D to override. Side assist uses the same gas reserve; you still control roll and the landing.

Touch down within 15° of upright and below 5 m/s. Excessive speed, sideways drift, spin, or a collision damages the car. The game starts at normal speed; slow motion is available for practice.
